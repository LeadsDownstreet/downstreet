Privacy policy

Downstreet helps homeowners in and around Fort Worth find a local pro. Downstreet Pro is the side of the same business that works with contractors. Both are run by Leads by Delta (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, and who sees it.

What we collect

When you ask for a pro

  • The kind of job, your ZIP code, your name and your mobile number.
  • Anything you choose to write about the job.

When a contractor applies or reports a job

  • Business name, contact name, phone, email and website.
  • Trade, shop ZIP code, service area, visit times, smallest job, and licensing and insurance status.
  • For job reports: the lead number, what happened, amounts collected and dates.

Sent automatically with a form

  • The page you sent it from, the site language, your browser type, how long the page was open, and the page that linked you to us.
  • If you arrived from an ad or a tagged link: the campaign tags in that link (for example the ad source, campaign name and click ID).
  • The exact call-and-text consent wording shown next to the button you tapped, so we have a record of what you agreed to.

We don’t ask for your street address on the site, and we never ask for payment details from homeowners.

How we use it

  • To call or text you about your request and match you with a local pro.
  • To review contractor applications and run our partner program, including tracking which jobs came from us and billing referral fees.
  • To see which ads and pages bring in requests, and to keep spam out.
  • To meet legal obligations and keep business records.

Who we share it with

  • The local pro we match you with. We send your request (name, number, ZIP and job details) to the pro we match you with for that job, so they can reach you. We don’t send your request to a list of companies.
  • Service providers that run our tools. Form entries are stored in Google Sheets and email alerts go through Gmail (Google). The site is hosted by GitHub, and the page fonts load from Google Fonts. If we turn on advertising tools such as Meta Pixel or Google Analytics, those companies receive information about your visit (see below).
  • When the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.
  • If the business is sold or reorganized, to the new owner, under this policy.

We don’t sell your personal information. We don’t share your mobile number or your consent to receive texts with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

Calls and texts

If you give us your number and tap the button, you agree that we and the pro we match you with may call or text you about your request, including by automated means. You don’t have to agree in order to buy anything. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts, or tell us on a call that you don’t want more calls.

Cookies, browser storage and ads

  • The site saves small settings in your browser, such as light or dark mode, and remembers the ad or link that brought you here for up to 30 days so a request can be credited to it.
  • We may use tools from Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google to measure our ads. When they’re on, they set cookies and tell those companies that someone visited a page or sent a request. You can limit this in your Facebook and Google ad settings, or by blocking third-party cookies in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep requests, applications and job reports as long as we need them to connect you with a pro, track the outcome of the job (contractor referrals count for 12 months), bill fees and keep business records. After that we delete them or strip out what identifies you.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email [EMAIL] or call [PHONE]. We may need to keep some records the law requires.

Security

We limit who can see form entries and use the security controls of the services above. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

Children

Our services are for adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
